MOIL GeoSync - SIH Presentation Guide
======================================
This document is designed to help you explain the MOIL GeoSync platform during your Smart India Hackathon (SIH) presentation. It explains what the app does, how the AI works, and the latest features we've added.

1. THE CORE PROBLEM WE ARE SOLVING
----------------------------------
MOIL (Manganese Ore India Limited) faces two massive challenges:
1. Exploration is expensive and slow. Finding new Manganese deposits takes years of manual surveying.
2. Mining operations are unpredictable. Rain, broken equipment, and bad fleet management cause huge production shortfalls and financial losses.

GeoSync solves this by using AI to predict WHERE to mine (Exploration) and HOW to mine efficiently (Production & Fleet Dispatch).

2. THE SIX MAIN DASHBOARDS
--------------------------

A. GeoProspect AI (The Map Dashboard)
What it does: It uses a Machine Learning model (Random Forest) to predict the exact probability of finding Manganese across MOIL's operational regions. 
Latest Updates: 
- We focused the AI strictly on MOIL's two primary operational belts: Central India - Maharashtra (Nagpur-Bhandara belt) and Madhya Pradesh (Balaghat-Bharweli belt).
- The map shows 10 real-world MOIL mining sites with exact GPS coordinates.
- It displays heatmaps for Prospectivity (chances of finding ore), NDVI (Vegetation cover), and Iron Oxide.
How to pitch it: "Instead of digging blindly, our AI analyzes geological data, satellite imagery, and soil moisture to pinpoint the exact coordinates where MOIL should drill next."

*** HOW TO DEMO THE MAP TO JUDGES (Step-by-Step) ***
Step 1: Start with "All Central India" selected. Point out the glowing Red/Orange "Prospectivity" heatmap across the Sausar belt. Tell the judges: "The AI has highlighted these exact hotspots as having an 80%+ probability of containing Manganese."
Step 2: Toggle ON the "Known Mines" layer. The map will overlay colored dots (Orange for Maharashtra, Cyan for Madhya Pradesh). Say: "As you can see, our AI's predictions perfectly align with MOIL's existing active mines (like Dongri Buzurg, Balaghat, and Ukwa), proving the model's accuracy."
Step 3: Change the Region dropdown to "Maharashtra (Nagpur-Bhandara)" or "Madhya Pradesh (Balaghat)" to zoom into state-level operations. "The model seamlessly navigates between MOIL's Maharashtra and Madhya Pradesh sectors."
Step 4: Scroll down to the "Top 10 Drill Targets" table. "Finally, the AI outputs a ranked list of precise GPS coordinates for the exploration team to investigate tomorrow morning."

B. Production Forecast
What it does: Predicts if a mine will actually hit its planned production target in the upcoming months.
How it works: A Gradient Boosting AI model looks at 10 years of historical data across MOIL's 10 mines. It factors in Rainfall, Equipment Availability, Blasting Days, and Haul Road Conditions.
How to pitch it: "If the monsoon season is approaching, our AI predicts the exact drop in production (TPD - Tons Per Day). If a mine is predicted to fall short, the system flags it as 'High Risk' months in advance so management can prepare."

*** HOW TO DEMO PRODUCTION FORECAST TO JUDGES (Step-by-Step) ***
Step 1: Point to the main line chart and say, "The blue line is MOIL's optimistic target, but the red line is what our AI predicts will ACTUALLY happen based on historical data."
Step 2: Change the Mine in the sidebar to a different location (e.g., 'Mine_D_Balaghat' in Madhya Pradesh or 'Mine_A_Dongri_Buzurg' in Maharashtra). Show how the AI dynamically predicts the impact during the monsoon months (July-Sept) due to heavy rainfall specific to that region.
Step 3: Point out the 'High Risk' alert boxes and explain: "Instead of being surprised by a sudden 30% drop in August, management now knows exactly which months will fail to hit targets, allowing them to proactively rent more equipment."
Step 4: Show the Feature Importance chart at the bottom. "The AI isn't a black box. It clearly shows the judges that 'equipment availability' and 'rainfall' are mathematically the biggest factors driving these shortfalls."

C. Intelligent Fleet Dispatch
What it does: Optimizes the movement of Dumpers (trucks) and Shovels (diggers) inside the mine.
How it works: It uses advanced mathematical optimization (Linear Programming via Google OR-Tools). If a shovel is sitting idle while another shovel has 5 trucks waiting in line, the AI automatically calculates the perfect reallocation to clear the bottleneck.
How to pitch it: "We don't just predict problems; we solve them on the ground. Our optimizer acts like an air traffic controller for the mine, telling the manager exactly which truck to move to which shovel to maximize daily throughput."

D. What-If Simulator
What it does: A sandbox environment where managers can play with variables.
How it works: You can use sliders to increase rainfall, decrease equipment availability, or change road conditions. The AI instantly updates the charts to show the impact.
Latest Updates: We massively increased the font sizes and styling here so it looks incredibly premium and readable on a big presentation screen.

E. Financial ROI (Return on Investment)
What it does: Turns operational data into money. 
How it works: If the AI predicts a 72,000-ton shortfall, this dashboard calculates exactly how much revenue is lost. It then proves mathematically whether MOIL should rent or buy new dumpers to cover the gap by showing the Break-Even Point and Payback Period.
How to pitch it: "Ultimately, this is about the bottom line. We prove to the stakeholders that investing in an AI-optimized fleet pays for itself in just a few months."

F. GenAI Assistant
What it does: A ChatGPT-like assistant built directly into the app.
How to pitch it: "Mine managers aren't data scientists. If they want to know 'Which mine has the highest risk next month?', they just type it in plain English, and our integrated GenAI reads the data and gives them the answer instantly."

3. BEHIND THE SCENES (The Tech Stack)
-------------------------------------
If the judges ask what technology you used:
- Frontend: Streamlit (Python) with custom CSS injections for a premium, dark-mode glassmorphism UI.
- Data Visualization: Plotly & Mapbox (for the interactive 3D heatmaps and charts).
- Machine Learning: Scikit-Learn (Random Forest & Gradient Boosting Regressors).
- Fleet Optimization: Google OR-Tools (Linear Programming solver).

4. HOW TO RUN THE DEMO SMOOTHLY
-------------------------------
- Everything is heavily cached for speed. If you ever change the code or data while the app is running and it doesn't update, click the three dots in the top right of the browser and click "Clear Cache" (or press 'C' on your keyboard).
- For the best look, make sure the browser is fullscreen (F11) to show off the UI scaling we implemented.
